The Ironwake Galley

A hot meal above Brasshaven.

Our restaurant is inside an old cargo airship at North Tower, Dock 07.

Welcome aboard

Captain Mabel Wrench turned her old airship into a place for crews and travelers to eat.

The kitchen uses steam-powered cookers. We serve beef, chicken, noodles, and a few vegetarian meals. You can sit by a window or choose a booth near the kitchen.

The ship stays docked during dinner, so you do not need a flight ticket.

See our menu or request a table.

Hours and location

Open: Every day, 4–10 p.m.

Find us: North Tower, Dock 07, Brasshaven.

Take the passenger lift to the dock. Tables seat 1–8 guests.
